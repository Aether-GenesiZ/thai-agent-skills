# Voice and editing choices

Adapted from kien-thai's frames, register and style guidance, and claude-thai-skills'
translation/customer-service conventions. These are context-sensitive choices.

- Explain: state the mechanism and consequence. Name the actor when responsibility
  could be ambiguous. Technical English is useful for a technical audience.
- News/reference: identify who did what and when. Avoid invented quotations, praise,
  personal address or chat particles.
- Marketing: address the reader consistently. Explain a real benefit supported by
  the brief; do not invent numbers, urgency, testimonials, or guarantees.
- Personal blog: use the author's established voice. A supplied first-person example
  does not authorize guessing another author's gender.
- Business email: state purpose, action, deadline and sign-off. Polite does not require
  `ขออนุญาต` before every sentence or repeated `ทางเรา`.
- Customer service: acknowledge the actual issue proportionately, then explain the
  next step. Do not promise refunds, delivery dates or policy exceptions not supplied.
- Official/academic: explicit roles, precise terminology and mandated structure may
  matter more than a conversational rhythm. `ซึ่ง`, `โดย`, passive voice and summaries
  can all be appropriate; inspect their meaning instead of deleting mechanically.

Avoid calques such as `มันเป็นสิ่งสำคัญที่จะ` when `ควร` or a concrete instruction
expresses the intended meaning. `ได้รับการอนุมัติ` is valid neutral Thai passive;
`ถูก` is not inherently restricted to negative events. Topic-first order is useful
when the topic is heavy; simple SVO Thai is also natural.

A before/after edit is good only if both versions retain the same facts. Added
specificity is not a style improvement if it invents a source, actor, date or result.
A brief summary may help a long technical document when the user wants it. Do not
force rhetorical questions, abrupt endings, slang, or a ban on all summaries.

Use `ๆ` with surrounding spacing in ordinary prose (`ต่าง ๆ`, `บ่อย ๆ`) unless
preserving a supplied style/quotation. Keep established spellings consistent,
for example `อินเทอร์เน็ต`, `อีเมล`, `แอป`, `เว็บไซต์`.

Typical edits (constructed examples):

- `ระบบได้ทำการบันทึกข้อมูลของคุณเป็นที่เรียบร้อยแล้ว` → `บันทึกข้อมูลแล้ว`
- `พวกเรามีความตื่นเต้นที่จะประกาศการเปิดตัว` → `วันนี้เปิดตัว…`
- `กรุณาทำการคลิกที่ปุ่มเพื่อดำเนินการต่อ` → `กดปุ่มเพื่อดำเนินการต่อ`
- `ขออภัยอย่างสูงสุดสำหรับความไม่สะดวก` → `ขออภัยในความไม่สะดวก`

Do not edit quoted phrases merely because they appear in a style blocklist.
